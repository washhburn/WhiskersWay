## Whisker's Way

This is my final project in a Software Development course. The game idea is my own, and the development happened with some help from tutorials online and AI. 
The game is about a cat that is lost in the world and wants to go back to their home. The player will encounter NPCs that need help with quests, navigate obstacles, combat enemies.
In every scene, the player needs to collect 5/5 quest items - once they've been found, an arrow will show the way to the transition point which send the player into the next scene.
The scenes are continuations of each other and together make a world map.


The game has several functions:

**Player Movement**:
The player moves with WASD-keys. The shift-key will increase the speed and lets the player run. Space-key will make the player jump, and thus jump over obstacles.

**Quest System**:
There is a main quest that the player need to finish in order to proceed to the next level - collect 5/5 quest items per scene to finish the game. There are also sidequests given by the NPCs, where you need to collect items for them or kill enemies. 

**NPC Dialogue**:
NPCs have their own dialogues and the dialogues change depending on Quest progress.  

**Inventory**:
Quest items are saved in the inventory of the player. Items collected in side quests are also visible in the inventory.

**Obstacles**:
There are three different enemies that the player encounters during gameplay - snakes, cobras and a boar. These enemies have different damage points and speed, which makes some enemies more difficult, but also more fun to play against.
The player also need to navigate through the different areas in the game to find the quest items. There are occasionally blocks that the player needs to jump over in order to reach the quest item.

**Powerups**:
To further enhance gameplay, enemies drop powerups each time they are hit. There are three powerups: Speed boost, damage boost and shield boost. As the names suggest, speed boost will increase the speed of the player, damage boost will allow damage to hit harder, and shield boost will increase the health of the player.

**Animation**:
All NPCs, enemies and the player have animations. These change depending on the direction the character is moving in.
